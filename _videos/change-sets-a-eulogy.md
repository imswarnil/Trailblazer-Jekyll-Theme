The talk itself: change sets deserve a fair obituary. They were slow, they
forgot dependencies, they could not be diffed, versioned or rolled back —
and for a decade they let a hundred thousand admins deploy on a Friday and
mostly get away with it. The tools that replaced them are better in every
measurable way, and this eulogy measures exactly what we lost anyway: a
deployment story you could teach in an afternoon.
