I started as the accidental admin at a retail company — the person who happened
to be nearest the org when it needed someone. That turned into Flow, which
turned into Apex, which turned into a decade of building on the platform for
other people.

These days I work mostly on large Service Cloud orgs and the integrations
hanging off them. The interesting problems are rarely the ones in the job
description: they are governor limits that only bite at month end, deployment
pipelines that nobody owns, and data models that made sense to someone in 2016.

## What I write about

Things that took me longer than they should have. If I spent a day working
something out, that is usually a day someone else is about to spend, and the
post is cheaper than the day.
