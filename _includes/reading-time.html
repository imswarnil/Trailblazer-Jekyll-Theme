{%- comment -%} READING TIME {% include reading-time.html content=page.content %} Words divided by a reading speed, rounded up, floor of one minute. Deliberately crude: a precise-looking "7.4 min" implies a measurement nobody made, and the only thing a reader wants from this number is "can I read it now or not". Set `reading.words_per_minute` in _config.yml, or `reading.show_reading_time: false` to drop it everywhere. {%- endcomment -%} {%- if site.reading.show_reading_time != false -%} {%- assign wpm = site.reading.words_per_minute | default: 220 -%} {%- assign words = include.content | strip_html | number_of_words -%} {%- assign minutes = words | divided_by: wpm -%} {%- if words > 0 and minutes < 1 %}{% assign minutes = 1 %}{% endif -%} {%- assign remainder = words | modulo: wpm -%} {%- if remainder > 0 and minutes >= 1 %}{% assign minutes = minutes | plus: 1 %}{% endif -%} {{- minutes }} min read {%- endif -%}
