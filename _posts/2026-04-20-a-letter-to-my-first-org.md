Dear org 00D4x0000012345,

I am sorry about the trigger. All four of them, on Account, each written in
a different year by a different version of me, each convinced it was the
last one anyone would need. I know now why the platform people talk about
one trigger per object the way they do — they had met me before I arrived.

I am sorry about `Amount2__c`. You know what you did, and so do I.

Thank you for the governor limits. I called them many things in 2017, none
printable, all wrong. They were the only code review I had that year — the
only voice in the building saying *this will not scale* to someone who did
not yet know the word "scale" could apply to him.

You taught me that every org is an archaeology site, that every weird field
had a reasonable Tuesday behind it, and that the person whose mess I was
cursing was usually me with less sleep. I have cursed gently ever since, in
your honour.

The new org says hello. It has one trigger per object. You would hate it.

— A.
