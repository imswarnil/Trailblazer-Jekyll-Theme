Four stars, not five, and the missing star is honest: a fifth of the book is
advice the industry has since absorbed so completely that reading it now feels
like being told to drink water. But the chapters on estimation, on tracer
bullets, and on the cost of broken windows are still the best short statements
of those ideas anywhere.

The "stone soup" essay alone has paid for the book several times over in
consulting work — it is the correct playbook for improving an org nobody has
given you permission to improve.

**Read it when:** early. This is the one to hand a first-year developer, which
is exactly what I keep doing with my copy, which is why I no longer have one.
