The rare platform book that ages well, because it teaches the *model* rather
than the menus. The chapters on execution context and static variables explain
about half of the "why did that fire twice" questions I have ever been asked,
and the design-pattern chapters are the source most trigger frameworks are
quietly paraphrasing.

**Read it when:** you can write a trigger that works and want to understand
why it works. Too early and the middle chapters slide past; at the right
moment they reorganise how you think about the platform.

**The one caveat:** examples predate some newer syntax, so copy the ideas
rather than the code. The ideas have not dated a day.
