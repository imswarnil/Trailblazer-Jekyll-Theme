This course exists because the gap it crosses is real and nobody owns it.
Admin content stops at Flow; developer content assumes you already know what a
compiler is. If you have ever built a forty-element Flow and thought "there
has to be a shorter way to say this" — there is, and it takes about two hours
to learn the shape of it.

## What you need

A free Developer Edition org, a laptop, and one honest admission: the canvas
was starting to fight you.

## What you will not get

A career change in two hours. This gets you to *reading* production Apex
comfortably and *writing* small triggers safely — which is the point where
every developer you work with becomes twice as useful to you, because you can
finally meet them halfway.
