A case study rather than a tutorial: one real console, eleven weeks, no feature
freeze, and an honest accounting of the two weeks we lost to a compatibility
shim we should have avoided.

The single slide people photographed: **Aura can contain LWC, LWC cannot
contain Aura** — which is the fact that dictates the entire migration order.

No video — the room had no capture setup, which I did not check beforehand
and would check next time. The audio above survived on a lapel recorder.
